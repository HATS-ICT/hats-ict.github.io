{% include section-heading.html number="01" title="Research" %}

A

Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

Training teams of agents that coordinate, navigate, and fight as a unit — from graph-based terrain reasoning and GNN policies to organizational hierarchies and emergent tactical behaviors like ambush and cover.

B

Cognitive Architectures & Human-like Behavior

Synthetic characters grounded in how people actually think — theory of mind, cognitive modeling with Sigma and PySigma, and decision-making that remains believable to the humans training alongside it.

C

LLM-driven Agents & Evaluation

Language models as reasoning engines for embodied agents — and rigorous benchmarks for what they can and cannot do, from gameplay understanding to deception detection and egocentric tactical reasoning.

D

Simulation & Scenario Generation

The environments where it all comes together — the RIDE rapid integration and development environment, geo-specific terrain, and automated generation of training scenarios.
